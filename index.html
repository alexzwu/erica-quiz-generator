How to use this planner

Episodes move through a pipeline: Idea → Outreach → Scheduled → Recorded → Editing → Published. Tap an episode to open it, and change its stage from the dropdown inside.

The + button adds an episode. Ideas is a scratch pad for topics — "Make episode" promotes an idea into the pipeline.

Inside an episode you can track the guest (contact details stay on this device), log every outreach touch, plan the recording, build the outline from segment blocks, keep research notes, and tick off the publish checklist. Paste the Spotify link once the episode is live.

Everything saves automatically on this device. If sync is enabled, a 🔒 bar appears at the top — unlock it with the family passphrase to sync across browsers.
